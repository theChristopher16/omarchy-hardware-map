# iPad mini 2 audio: a related A5 reference

This is a comparison of separate projects, not a new iPad mini 2 audio test.

[Project Cascadia's authors report speaker playback on an A5 iPad mini 1](https://github.com/rzxvx/Project-Cascadia/blob/e8f3e045a5e38bde5a5a79e1cc72ae22020d63ac/docs/research/p105-audio.md), using amplifiers they identify as CS35L19. In an earlier mini 2 experiment, this project recorded responses from two devices it associates with CS35L19 amplifiers; that association was not independently verified against a vendor identification reference in this review. The documented A5 audio route differs from the mini 2's recorded topology. The mini 1 report does not establish mini 2 speaker playback, microphone capture, or suitability of the mini 1's board settings.

The [mini 2 Linux audio inventory](https://github.com/theChristopher16/omarchy-hardware-map/blob/72750e27010dba37eff324011b14dabb290aef41/evidence/ipad-mini2-j85/2026-10-27-audio.md) did not find a sound card or PCM on its inspected RAM boot. The later [PCM guard build attempt](https://github.com/theChristopher16/omarchy-hardware-map/blob/dd956adff92b71517d6ac7e7a7ca94f3eda16fd2/evidence/ipad-mini2-j85/2026-10-28-pcm-guard-build-attempt.md) remains an offline attempt with no verified iPad playback or capture.

# Orchard A10 Mesa updates and J85 boundary — 3 November 2026 UTC

At the checked Orchard Mesa head [73a49ab](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/mesa/commit/73a49abe24726c703ff32f060a9b7cd0c4444ce2), the fork is eight commits ahead of the prior saved 2e3c9e6. The contributor reports that [host-image-copy work](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/mesa/commit/6ccd31a3e036a80eba5a9cdb72857d523fbcdfc7) passed 15 of 15 Vulkan triangle cases and several glmark2 workloads through Zink without a GPU reset on the iPad 6/J71b A10. A separate [DMA-BUF change](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/mesa/commit/2d2e29774ee516bf049cdf1bb9910fa07d01e09b) and [Omarchy A compositor patch](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/omarchy-a/commit/8470c0a77d80abf216b552c8fb0a73ec7d56e566) report application/compositor tests and a fallback path on J71b. These are contributor reports; this review did not independently run them.

This research concerns the A10 G9P path. It may inform future Mesa and desktop integration, but does not establish that the A7/J85 uses the same GPU implementation, firmware or device settings. [Orchard's A10 Mesa notes](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/gpu/blob/c263b4649d00be60618f4c6d9340731c885437ef/a10/MESA.md) also document incomplete driver/application behavior.

Earlier J85 runtime9 work recorded a short power-domain transition and a CPU-only synthetic encoder comparison; neither is GPU execution. See the [previous J85 evidence](https://github.com/theChristopher16/omarchy-hardware-map/blob/faf5fa3de2c67ee8180dcfa3cb2063ea6eddf36f/evidence/ipad-mini2-j85/2026-11-01-a10-runtime9-build.md). J85 GPU identity, firmware, a successful job, and an accelerated frame remain unverified.
